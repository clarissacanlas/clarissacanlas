Chainmaille Handpiece

June 2025

Jump rings, clasps, pliers, binder ring

For the past two years, I've been really into medieval imagery, and I try to incorporate elements into my customizable belongings. I wear this handpiece every day, and whenever I look down at my hands, I am reminded to be strong. Despite being tedious and finicky, the process was meditative for me. It is a European 4-in-1 weave.
